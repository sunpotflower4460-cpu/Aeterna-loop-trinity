# v2.2 Relation Maintenance Trace pre-registration

## Purpose

Relation Maintenance Trace pre-registers the minimal trace schema, first-guardrail event taxonomy, terminal outcomes, and claim discipline for future RMT implementation. This PR is design-only. It does not implement a runner, generate measured artifacts, or change runtime physics.

Relation Maintenance Trace is a finite-horizon observer plan. It is not a new physical mechanism. It asks what happens after structurally distinct A/B enter a provisional relation band or become control-separated: whether they remain distinguishable, collapse into identity, make memory a current copy, detach memory, lose field health, hit ledger or winding reliability limits, or show energy instability first.

Meeting is not maintenance.

## Why RMT comes before drive / N-field work

RMT is the calibration ruler for relation maintenance. Before adding drive, gentle-pulse wiring, or N-field runtime changes, the project must first record how current G8-like and related finite-horizon conditions meet, leave relation bands, or hit guardrails.

The first RMT version should remain minimal. Relation Event Time, Internal Time Pause, Memory Kernel Axis, and HISTORY_ALPHA x GAMMA grid sweeps are important follow-up ideas, but they belong primarily to a later time/memory gate unless explicitly promoted by a later pre-registration.

## Provisional guardrails

All provisional guardrails are heuristic band, not yet calibrated. They are trace interpretation guardrails, not success criteria. These are guardrails for finite-horizon trace interpretation. They are not proof thresholds.

- `identityCollapseGuardrail`: `alignedFieldABDistance < 0.005`.
- `nearIdentityCollapseGuardrail`: `alignedFieldABDistance < 0.01`.
- `provisionalMeetingBand`: `alignedFieldABDistance` between `0.01` and `0.15`.
- `memoryCopyCollapseGuardrail`: `memoryFieldDifferenceA` or `memoryFieldDifferenceB < 0.05`.
- `memoryDetachedGuardrail`: `memoryFieldDifferenceA` or `memoryFieldDifferenceB > 0.35`.
- `fieldFlatteningGuardrail`: `amplitudeStdA` or `amplitudeStdB` too low; exact threshold to be inherited from existing audit/runtime conventions or left to implementation PR.
- `energyInstabilityGuardrail`: energy proxy changes outside pre-registered tolerance; exact tolerance is not calibrated in this design PR.
- `ledgerReliabilityLimited`: `invalidPlaquetteCount > 0` or near-pi / low-amplitude reliability flags indicate ledger cannot support topology-like classification.
- `windingReliabilityLimited`: `invalidLineCount > 0` or near-pi phase-step flags indicate x-line winding cannot support classification.
- `pheromoneFogGuardrail`: `pheromoneActiveRatio` / entropy indicates global fog rather than local trace; exact threshold is not calibrated in this design PR.

- `no_meeting_observed`

- `control_separated_but_reliability_limited`
- `short_horizon_maintenance_candidate`
- `boundary`
- `indeterminate`

Rules:

- `short_horizon_maintenance_candidate` is not long-horizon maintenance.
- `meeting_without_maintenance` is not a project failure; it is a measured outcome.
- `control_separated_but_reliability_limited` is used when relation/distance axes show measurable difference from control, but observer reliability prevents stronger classification.
- `boundary` is used when distance, memory, topology, energy, or field-health axes conflict.
- `indeterminate` is used when instrumentation is insufficient.

Forbidden terminal outcomes:

- `success`
- `proof`
- `life_like`
- `survival`
- `consciousness`
- `agency`
- `soul`
- `permanent`

## Meeting vs maintenance rule

Meeting is not maintenance.

A/B entering a provisional relation band at one or more sampled steps is a meeting event. Maintenance requires remaining distinguishable and interpretable across a finite horizon without identity collapse, memory copy collapse, memory detachment, field flattening, energy instability, or reliability-limited observer failure. Even then, it is only a finite-horizon candidate, not long-horizon maintenance proof.
