# v2.2 Structurally Distinct Beings Interaction Smoke

## Purpose

This document records the first G8 Structurally Distinct Beings Interaction Smoke. The G8 artifacts are finite-horizon operational smoke artifacts.

## Scope

The smoke generates compact diagnostic artifacts only. It does not modify runtime physics, Observer V2, Topological Ledger, energy metrics, package files, or lockfiles. The original implementation attempted the G8 vessel correctly but required memory coupling so coupled conditions actually act through the implemented runtime path.

## Relation to G8 pre-registration

The runner follows `docs/g8-structurally-distinct-interaction-preregistration.md` from PR #41. Ledger agreement is not automatically success, and ledger mismatch is not automatically failure.

## Operational meaning of “beings”

“Beings” is an operational project label for structurally distinct field configurations tracked over a finite horizon. It does not imply biological life, consciousness, agency, subjectivity, s-o-u-l, or permanent survival. The word “beings” is retained only to match the pre-registered artifact lineage and does not upgrade the claim level.

## Families and conditions

Family T is a transient short-horizon meeting smoke, not maintenance. Conditions: `T-control-g0`, `T-coupled-g-provisional`, and `T-coupled-detuning-outside-provisional`.

Family P is a persistent finite-horizon maintenance-candidate smoke, not permanent survival. Conditions: `P-control-g0-W1xW0`, `P-coupled-g-provisional-W1xW0`, `P-boundary-low-g-provisional-W1xW0`, and `P-boundary-high-g-provisional-W1xW0`.

## Matched controls

Every coupled condition records a matched `g=0` control in the same family. Family T coupled runs map to `T-control-g0-run`; Family P coupled runs map to `P-control-g0-W1xW0-run`.

## Δω policy

Structurally distinct detuning boundaries are not calibrated. Detuning references are recorded as provisional smoke context or `not_calibrated_for_structurally_distinct_layouts`; v2.1.2 detuning scan context is not transferred as a settled G8 boundary. All relation-band thresholds remain heuristic / not yet calibrated.

## Initializer provenance

The runner uses `createRandomizedAeternaFields` with `phaseConstructionMode: "periodic-dipole-image-sum-radius-1"` for structurally distinct layout fixtures. Seed-only fixed-layout noise differences are not treated as structural distinction. Artifacts record compact `vortexLayoutA`, `vortexLayoutB`, layout relation, pair count, min separation ratio, net charges, and carrier provenance. Family P applies a manual global x-winding ramp on top of randomized layouts.

## Observer V2 axis

Samples report raw and gauge-aligned field/memory A/B distances, theta-star values, work proxy, validity amplitude threshold, near-π metrics, and amplitude/reliability guardrails using existing Observer V2 utilities.

## Topological Ledger axis

Samples report positive and negative plaquette counts, total winding, net charge, A/B plaquette-map distance, distance to control when available, ledger deltas, invalid plaquettes, near-π edges, and first agreement/disagreement fields. Ledger identity is diagnostic only, not a success gate.

## Energy-balance context

Family P records `energyBalanceSummary` and `energy_balance_confound_possible`. Energy-balance context is a confound check, not a success axis. Coupling effectiveness is also a mechanical precondition, not evidence of success.

## Classification taxonomy

Classifications are trace-derived and use registered labels such as `common_vacuum_relaxation`, `phase_drift_with_interaction`, `phase_locking_with_structural_preservation`, `structural_identity_collapse`, and `indeterminate`. Boundary is primarily an `outcomeCategory`; prose interpretation belongs in `classificationNotes`.
